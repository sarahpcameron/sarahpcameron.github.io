The first paper from my dissertation is published in Brain Research and can be accessed [here](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brainres.2026.150390). The paper is titled "Linear distance modulates P600 amplitude for gender agreement: An ERP study in Norwegian" and investigates the role of linear distance on processing of gender agreement in L1 speakers.
